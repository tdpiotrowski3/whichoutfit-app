Affiliate Disclosure

How shopping links work in WhichOutfit, and how we get paid when they're used.

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Disclosure: WhichOutfit works with affiliate networks, such as Rakuten Advertising, and with retailers directly. When you buy something through a store link in the app, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and the price you pay is the store's price.

When you'll see store links

WhichOutfit styles you from the clothes you already own. Store links show up only when a look is missing a piece. The stylist describes that piece (color, fabric and cut), and the app shows a few matching products from retailers' public listings, each with its photo, price and store name. You can see the flow on How It Works.

How picks are chosen

  • Products are ranked by how closely they match the piece the stylist described, your style profile and the weather or occasion. Whether a link pays a commission doesn't change the order.
  • No retailer can pay to be shown, to rank higher or to be described more favorably.
  • A piece you already own always comes before a shopping suggestion.

How links are labeled

Any screen that shows a link that may earn us a commission carries a visible "Paid links" note. Tapping a product opens the retailer's own page in a browser. Prices, stock, shipping and returns are set by that retailer, and your purchase is between you and them.

What we don't do

  • No coupon codes, cash-back offers or browser extensions.
  • No ads placed on search results or on other websites.
  • No rewards in exchange for clicking or buying.

Your data

When you open an affiliate link, the network and the retailer record the click so the sale can be credited. They may set their own cookies once you're on the retailer's page. We never send them your name, email or closet. See our Privacy Policy for details.
